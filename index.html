How to Verify Offline Operation:

  1. Click Download & Cache Model below while connected to the internet.
  2. Wait until all checkmarks turn green and the status shows Offline Ready.
  3. Disconnect your Wi-Fi or open Browser DevTools → Network tab → select Offline.
  4. Refresh the page (F5) — the app will load from the Service Worker and respond locally without any internet!
